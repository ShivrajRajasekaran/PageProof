## 16. How our thresholds are chosen (and why not other values)
- **Status:** ✅, backed by measured data in `logs/GROUNDING_SWEEP.md` and `logs/RESULTS.md`
- **Where:** `_quote_supported()` (grounding threshold 0.8) and `CONF_THRESHOLD = 0.4` in `rapidagent/agent.py`. The sweeps come from `grounding_sweep.py` and `make_report.py`, and are explained on deck slide 7.
- **Judged component:** M, E
- **Talking point:**
  - **Grounding gate 0.8 is the threshold that decides answer vs "insufficient information".** We replayed every benchmark answer against the pages the agent read.
    - Anything from 0.5 to 0.8 keeps all correctly quoted answers. 0.9 or verbatim-only (1.0) wrongly blocks 1–2 more correct answers.
    - 0.8 is therefore the strictest value with no loss, and the strictness is what makes invented quotes fail.
  - **Confidence 0.4 is only a backstop.** Answering pays when p > λ/(1+λ), and 0.4 corresponds to a wrong answer costing about ⅔ of a right one.
    - We measured qwen3:8b at 0.95–1.0 on every answer, including wrong ones, so confidence alone can't be trusted. That's the reason the quote check exists.
  - **Honest limit:** a real but outdated quote (a contradiction case) passes any quote threshold, so contradictions are handled separately.
